Leading Real Estate Agency in Lekki

Sachio Properties is a full-service real estate agency rooted in the heart of Lekki, Lagos — connecting discerning clients with verified homes, commercial spaces, and investment-grade land across Lekki-Ajah, Ikoyi, and the wider Lagos corridor.

From first enquiry to final keys, we handle every detail: vetting every listing, negotiating transparent deals, and staying reachable around the clock. Your dream home isn't just a transaction for us — it's a promise delivered.
